# OpenReloading

Open hardware and software for people who reload their own ammunition. We build tools to plan loads, simulate them and automate the bench, and we publish them for anyone to use, build and change.

**Plan and simulate.** [Ballistics](https://github.com/OpenReloading/Ballistics) is a database of cartridges with interior and exterior ballistics simulations: the pressure and muzzle velocity of a load, the bullet's trajectory, chronograph statistics and shot groups. Every record names its sources.

**Build the bench.** Hardware projects come with build guides: a pneumatic press drive on 24 V DC relay logic (bill of materials, wiring, tubing) and a parametric brass and bullet collator built in code.

Results are simulations, not load data. Start from the load data your component makers publish.
